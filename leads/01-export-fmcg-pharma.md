## How to find more in this segment

1. **EDB Exporters Directory (Sri Lanka Export Development Board)** - searchable by tea, coconut, spices, processed food, ayurveda, pharma; each profile has address and website. https://www.srilankabusiness.com/exporters-directory/
2. **Sri Lanka Tea Board (registered exporters, GMP list, trade-fair pavilion exhibitor lists)** and **Tea Exporters Association** committee/member pages. https://srilankateaboard.lk/ and https://teasrilanka.org/committee-members
3. **Coconut Development Authority exporter registrations (searchable by product)** - full list of DC, VCO, coconut milk exporters with addresses. https://www.cda.gov.lk/web/index.php?option=com_cda&view=exporter_registrations&limit=0&search=&product_id=&lang=en
4. **Department of Cinnamon Development / Spice Council** - cinnamon exporter registrations and industry events. https://cinnamon.gov.lk/eng/
5. **NCE Export Awards and Presidential Export Awards winner lists** - annual sector-by-sector winners (tea, spices, coconut, food, ayurveda). https://nce.lk/exporter-awards/ and https://pea.edb.gov.lk/
6. **Sri Lanka Pharmaceutical Manufacturers' Association (SLPMA)** members and Sri Lanka Chamber of the Pharmaceutical Industry (SLCPI). https://www.slpma.lk/ and https://lk.linkedin.com/company/sri-lanka-chamber-of-the-pharmaceutical-industry
7. **Tender portals**: SPC global tenders, SPMC tenders, and TendersOnTime SPC feed (flag print/packaging and label tenders). https://www.spc.lk/tender-display.php , https://spmc.gov.lk/media/tenders , https://www.tendersontime.com/sri-lanka/authority/state-pharmaceuticals-corporation-of-sri-lanka-tenders-42/
8. **Trade shows**: Sri Lanka Tea Board pavilions at FOODEX Japan and Hong Kong International Tea Fair (exhibitor lists name active exporters); Fine Food Australia Sri Lanka pavilion (MFA); Profood Propack Agbiz (Colombo, annual food and packaging expo). https://srilankateaboard.lk/news-and-events/ and https://www.mfa.gov.lk/en/news/sri-lanka-showcases-first-ever-pavilion-fine-food-australia-2026
9. **Kompass Sri Lanka - Ayurvedic medicines and formulas** category for smaller herbal manufacturers. https://lk.kompass.com/a/ayurvedic-medicines-formulas/2316018/
10. **Supermarket and e-commerce brand listings** (Kapruka brand index, Cargills/Keells online) to spot new SKUs and rebrands. https://www.kapruka.com/online_brands.jsp
11. **News feeds for triggers**: Lanka Business News FMCG category, Daily FT Business, EconomyNext, Adaderana Biz. https://www.lankabusinessnews.com/category/fmcg/ and https://www.ft.lk/business
12. **LinkedIn search strings** (run at https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/companies/): `"Sri Lanka" ("tea exporter" OR "Ceylon tea") packaging`; `"Sri Lanka" ("coconut" OR "cinnamon" OR "spices") exporter`; `"Sri Lanka" FMCG ("brand manager" OR "packaging development" OR "procurement")`; `"Sri Lanka" ("pharmaceutical" OR "ayurveda") ("manufacturing" OR "packaging")`; people search: `"Sri Lanka" "packaging development" OR "packaging procurement"`.

Questions about brochures, catalogues & marketing print

What paper is best for a brochure?

170 gsm art paper is the popular choice for folded brochures. Use 250–300 gsm for single-sheet flyers that must feel premium, and 300–350 gsm for folders.

Can you design the brochure too?

Yes. Our in-house design team creates brochures, catalogues and packaging artwork, or prepares your agency's files for press.

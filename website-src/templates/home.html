A printing company in Sri Lanka built for brands that export, retail and grow

{{ site.brand }} is a printing and packaging company in Colombo serving customers across Sri Lanka, including Gampaha, Kalutara, Kandy, Galle, Negombo and the Katunayake and Biyagama export processing zones. We manufacture custom printed boxes, folding cartons, corrugated shipping cartons, mailer boxes, self-adhesive labels, shrink sleeves, rigid gift boxes, paper bags and food packaging, and we print brochures, catalogues, annual reports, books, business cards, stationery, banners, hoardings and signage.

Our customers include Ceylon tea exporters, apparel manufacturers, food and FMCG brands, pharmaceutical companies, ayurvedic and cosmetics brands, hotels and restaurants, banks and insurers, schools and universities, property developers and e-commerce sellers. Whether you need 50 boxes for a launch or a monthly carton programme, we offer the same colour-managed quality, free structural design and dependable delivery.

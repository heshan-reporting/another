Print-Ready Artwork Checklist

Tick every line before you send files to press. Nine out of ten delays happen in prepress, and this list prevents all of them.

  • 1. BleedBackground colour and images extend 3 mm beyond the trim line on every side.
  • 2. Safe zoneText, logos and barcodes sit at least 3–5 mm inside the trim line (10 mm on packaging folds).
  • 3. ResolutionImages are 300 dpi at final size (150 dpi is acceptable for large-format print).
  • 4. Colour modeEverything is CMYK. Spot colours are named Pantone swatches. No RGB, no Lab.
  • 5. Rich blackLarge black areas use C60 M40 Y40 K100. Small text and thin lines use K100 only.
  • 6. FontsAll text converted to outlines, or fonts packaged with the file. Body text ≥ 6 pt, legal text ≥ 4 pt.
  • 7. DielineArtwork is placed on the printer's dieline on its own non-printing spot-colour layer, set to overprint.
  • 8. OverprintOverprint preview checked. No white objects set to overprint. Knockouts where needed.
  • 9. BarcodesEAN/UPC ≥ 80% magnification, quiet zones intact, dark bars on light background, verified.
  • 10. TransparencyTransparency and effects flattened or exported as PDF/X-4. No missing links.
  • 11. Page setupSingle pages in reading order, correct trim size, no marks inside the artwork area, spreads exported as single pages.
  • 12. ExportPDF/X-4 or PDF/X-1a, crop marks on, 3 mm bleed, fonts embedded, no security or passwords.

Packaging extras

  • Glue flaps are free of ink and varnish unless the printer confirms a glueable coating.
  • Artwork on adjacent panels reads correctly when folded; make a paper mock-up.
  • Window positions match the die; mandatory information fits the panel space.
